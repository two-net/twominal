Keyboard Shortcuts

twominal

Your shell, with fish-style help built in. No fish required.

  • Suggestions

    As you type, the likely rest of the command appears dimmed. Press → to take it.

  • Completions

    Press ⇥ to complete commands, paths, options and git branches, each with a short description.

  • Themes

    Twominal follows your Mac’s light or dark appearance. Pick Dark or Light in Settings to keep one.

  • Vim

    Turn on Vim keys for the command line in Settings, and press ⇧⌘C to move through scrollback the Vim way.
